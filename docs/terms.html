These terms apply when you use Social Studio, the video publishing workspace maintained by the BaoHQ project.

Using Social Studio

Social Studio runs on your computer. You can use it to organize videos, prepare captions, manage your posting plan and send videos to platforms you configure and authorize. You are responsible for keeping your installation, credentials and connected accounts secure.

Your accounts and content

Use only accounts and Pages that you own or have permission to manage. You retain your rights to your content. By confirming a delivery, you instruct Social Studio to send the selected file and any applicable post details to the selected platform at the requested time.

Upload only content you have the right to use. Follow applicable laws and each platform’s rules, including rules on copyright, disclosures, audience settings and prohibited content.

Deliveries and public posts

Review the selected channel, caption, timing and audience before confirming a delivery. A YouTube or Facebook delivery can result in a public post. The TikTok integration sends a video draft to your TikTok inbox for you to finish and publish in TikTok.

Scheduled deliveries require Social Studio to be running and the relevant platform to accept your credentials. Platform processing, outages or limits can delay or prevent delivery. If the result is uncertain, check the platform before sending the video again.

Changes and questions

When these terms change, the updated text and effective date will appear on this page. For general questions, use project support on GitHub. GitHub issues are public, so leave out login credentials and personal information.
