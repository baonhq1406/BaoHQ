Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Social Studio, maintained by the BaoHQ project, handles information in its local installation and on these public policy pages.

Information stored on your computer

  • Video workspace. Social Studio stores uploaded videos, generated previews, titles, captions, schedules, delivery history and any post links you save. Videos found in your project folders are referenced in their original location.
  • Delivery records. The app stores the selected platform, scheduled time, delivery result and any post or upload identifier returned by the platform. It uses these records to run the queue and check delivery status.
  • Credentials. You configure platform credentials locally. The app keeps refreshed TikTok tokens in a local file so it can finish authorized deliveries and check their status.
  • Display preferences. Your browser stores your choice of list or grid layout for the local app.

Information sent to connected platforms

When you confirm a delivery, Social Studio uses your credentials to send the selected MP4 and the details required for that platform. YouTube and Facebook receive the title and caption as part of their publishing requests. TikTok receives the video and file details for an inbox draft. You finish the post and add the caption in TikTok.

The app also uses the relevant platform API to refresh credentials and check delivery status. Video uploads and platform accounts are governed by the provider’s privacy policy. See TikTok, Google and YouTube, and Meta and Facebook.

Retention and removal

Workspace data and delivery records remain in your local installation until removed. To clear all local workspace data, stop Social Studio and remove the data directory you configured. The default is social-studio/data/. This also removes the queue, app-managed uploads and any cached TikTok tokens. Original source files and backups are managed separately.

To disconnect a platform, revoke the app’s access in that platform and remove its credentials from your local environment. Manage videos already sent, including TikTok drafts and public posts, through the platform that received them.

Public policy pages

These pages have no forms for submitting workspace data. GitHub hosts them and logs visitor IP addresses for security, as described in its GitHub Pages documentation.

Questions and updates

Use project support on GitHub for general privacy questions. Issues are public. Keep personal information, unpublished videos and credentials out of them. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
